# Financial reading

Financials opens with a summary row and exactly three main charts: **How sales become profit**, **Sales over time**, and **What it owns and owes**. The summary and history share the selected **Past 12 months** or **Fiscal year** basis and date; the income-flow chart labels its own fiscal period. Assets/liabilities and cash/borrowing each use a shared signed scale and one selected balance date. Their amounts overlap and must not be added together.

**More detail** contains revenue segments, interpretation details, borrowing history, liquidity, balance-sheet composition, all reported figures, originals, filing checks, monitoring inputs and definitions. Date/series/basis choices survive tab changes; changing company resets them. Underlined figures and reading titles open readable input amounts, exact dates, calculations and original report links, including both current/prior growth inputs. There is no repeated Evidence button on each financial card. Original raw-figure disclosures and income-flow figure controls remain.

Competitor charts and the editable group live only in **Compare & value**. Financials has a single sentence and Compare link based on the same saved SEC annual-growth validation and fiscal-end proximity rules. Missing or incompatible peers are excluded from the stated denominator; no values, rank or peer choices are invented. It reads existing local data only. See [topic-homes verification](../reviews/2026-10-09/topic-homes.md).

`GET /api/v1/companies/{id}/financial-story` uses a read-only consistent transaction over the retained SEC payload, with the original `sec-companyfacts` permission. `sec-financial-story-2` projects up to twelve distinct annual periods and forty distinct annual/quarter report dates. The latest amendment for a period remains the displayed version, including missing figures; older snapshots and methods are never rewritten. No migration, provider/model request, allowance, watch or monitoring definition changes.

Annual and trailing histories remain separate. Trailing series reuse the existing fiscal-date/same-concept bridge. A bridge retains its known fiscal window when differing reporting labels withhold the amount. Displayed flow measures match the selected revenue dates when those dates are known; absent revenue dates do not erase independently valid income or cash flow. Each measure retains its own dates, inputs and original report links. Missing selected values show their specific reason beside the figure. Consolidated `ProfitLoss` and parent-only `NetIncomeLoss` stay distinct; a conflicting consolidated result is not hidden with parent income. Free cash flow subtracts nonnegative cash capital spending from matching operating cash flow. Net and operating margins use positive matching revenue; interest comparison requires specifically reported non-operating interest expense. Ratios use 28 significant decimal digits; input decimals remain unchanged and exact subtraction uses sufficient precision. Unsupported calculated precision withholds that calculation. See [historical Apple gap verification](../reviews/2026-10-09/aapl-history.md).

Overview labels its existing annual revenue growth **last fiscal year**, showing both compared year-end dates. The Financials sales-growth reading labels its selected basis **Fiscal year** or **Past 12 months**, with the exact current and prior start/end dates beside the figure. Changing Fiscal year/Past 12 months updates that label with the existing selected series. This presentation does not change growth arithmetic, inputs, compatible-period checks or evidence. The two readings can therefore differ when they describe different windows. See [content-first layout and period verification](../reviews/2026-10-09/content-first-ui.md).

Balance rows are instantaneous USD facts from the exact selected filing. Current and noncurrent totals remain separate. A missing noncurrent total may be labelled assets minus current assets, or liabilities minus current liabilities; a conflicting direct total is not replaced. Book equity is assets minus liabilities including non-controlling interests, not market equity. Borrowing uses the existing complete combined-debt convention and excludes leases. Borrowing/equity requires positive book equity. Current assets versus current liabilities does not establish payment ability. Cash versus borrowing does not assume cash is unrestricted. Cash flow versus borrowing is a dated flow/stock relationship, not a repayment schedule. A lower borrowing/equity ratio does not prove repayment. No earnings-quality, credit-health or investment rating is invented.

Line charts use one shared signed dollar scale including zero, actual fiscal dates and straight segments. Missing rows, a year gap and changes in concept/result definition break the path; three bridge inputs do not alone create a definition change. Legend toggles, the keyboard date slider, selected values and exact evidence remain available. Short-/long-term pairs share one zero-based scale. Positive balance-sheet blocks must reconcile to assets; each rectangle’s area is proportional to its amount. Missing categories remain in explicitly unclassified residuals, never silently zero. Negative equity or unreconciled parts suppress the positive-area breakdown while raw figures remain available. The complete labelled legend supports small blocks and phone layouts.

The page loads saved history only while selected, retains date/basis choices across navigation and fences late reads. An updated workspace payload cannot display a previous payload’s series. Source withdrawal hides the data. Evidence dialogs use compact mil/bil/tril amounts, explicit period cards and direct original-report links. Current/prior periods appear side by side on desktop and stack on phones. Calculation inputs sit behind **How this is calculated**, with human labels and their own dates. SEC concept names, accession strings, internal snapshot IDs and raw supporting tables are not rendered. Exact input decimals and amendment support remain unchanged in the retained source data; formatting never feeds calculations. Source links preserve each original filing vintage and segment fact anchor. Missing values, negatives, zero, periods and amendment status remain explicit. See [evidence polish verification](../reviews/2026-10-09/evidence-polish.md). Phones scroll charts within the page, and native keyboard/focus/reduced-motion behaviour remains.

## General retained-data coverage — 9 October 2026

Financial history, depth and income flow now share corrected read-time rules. A revenue bridge may select a common concept only when every original input is explicitly present under that same concept with identical amount/date/currency/accession. This does not equate different concepts. Interest uses the actual `InterestExpenseNonoperating` spelling; broader total interest remains separate.

If the preferred current-period category is absent, cash spending on productive assets can supply capital spending and FCF with explicit software/intangible wording; the complete same-definition bridge is still required. Labelled broader/narrower balance categories can supply accounts-and-other receivables, trade payables, total intangibles excluding goodwill and PP&E including finance-lease assets. A conflicting, wrong-currency or otherwise unusable preferred current-period fact blocks substitution. Residual composition values recalculate; no category is counted twice.

Explicit current plus noncurrent liabilities can form an absent total. Borrowing retains direct totals and complete original components first; when these are incomplete, a complete debt-and-finance-lease total minus an explicitly reported finance-lease liability can establish borrowing excluding leases. All inputs share the same filing/date; unsupported, negative or incomplete inputs stay unknown. Parent equity, partial borrowing and missing categories do not become consolidated totals or zero.

The amendment resolver also supports a tightly constrained annual MD&A-only declaration: explicit sole purpose and unchanged other items, Item7 content (optionally the Item15 index), no Item8 amendment or note indicating financial-statement changes/restatement, plus the original full chain/hash/permission/conflict checks. Source and amendment links remain attached. Income-flow calculations with retained amendment inputs reject mixed filing vintages. Resolver version participates in the source basis so stale cached evidence is invalidated. Original performance/model/monitoring/valuation records are unchanged.
