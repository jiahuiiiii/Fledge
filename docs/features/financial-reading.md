# Financial reading

Financials opens with a summary row and exactly three main charts: **How sales become profit**, **Sales over time**, and **What it owns and owes**. The summary and history share the selected **Past 12 months** or **Fiscal year** basis and date; the income-flow chart labels its own fiscal period. Assets/liabilities and cash/borrowing each use a shared signed scale and one selected balance date. Their amounts overlap and must not be added together.

**More detail** contains revenue segments, interpretation details, borrowing history, liquidity, balance-sheet composition, all reported figures, originals, filing checks, monitoring inputs and definitions. Date/series/basis choices survive tab changes; changing company resets them. Underlined figures and reading titles open exact inputs, dates, formulas and filing links, including both current/prior growth inputs. There is no repeated Evidence button on each financial card. Original raw-figure disclosures and income-flow figure controls remain.

Competitor charts and the editable group live only in **Compare & value**. Financials has a single sentence and Compare link based on the same saved SEC annual-growth validation and fiscal-end proximity rules. Missing or incompatible peers are excluded from the stated denominator; no values, rank or peer choices are invented. It reads existing local data only. See [topic-homes verification](../reviews/2026-10-09/topic-homes.md).

`GET /api/v1/companies/{id}/financial-story` uses a read-only consistent transaction over the retained SEC payload, with the original `sec-companyfacts` permission. `sec-financial-story-1` projects up to twelve distinct annual periods and forty distinct annual/quarter report dates. The latest amendment for a period remains the displayed version, including missing figures; older snapshots and methods are never rewritten. No migration, provider/model request, allowance, watch or monitoring definition changes.

Annual and trailing histories remain separate. Trailing series reuse the existing fiscal-date/same-concept bridge. Every displayed flow measure must match the selected revenue dates. Consolidated `ProfitLoss` and parent-only `NetIncomeLoss` stay distinct; a conflicting consolidated result is not hidden with parent income. Free cash flow subtracts nonnegative cash capital spending from matching operating cash flow. Net and operating margins use positive matching revenue; interest comparison requires specifically reported non-operating interest expense. Ratios use 28 significant decimal digits; input decimals remain unchanged and exact subtraction uses sufficient precision. Unsupported calculated precision withholds that calculation.

Overview labels its existing annual revenue growth **last fiscal year**, showing both compared year-end dates. The Financials sales-growth reading labels its selected basis **Fiscal year** or **Past 12 months**, with the exact current and prior start/end dates beside the figure. Changing Fiscal year/Past 12 months updates that label with the existing selected series. This presentation does not change growth arithmetic, inputs, compatible-period checks or evidence. The two readings can therefore differ when they describe different windows. See [content-first layout and period verification](../reviews/2026-10-09/content-first-ui.md).

Balance rows are instantaneous USD facts from the exact selected filing. Current and noncurrent totals remain separate. A missing noncurrent total may be labelled assets minus current assets, or liabilities minus current liabilities; a conflicting direct total is not replaced. Book equity is assets minus liabilities including non-controlling interests, not market equity. Borrowing uses the existing complete combined-debt convention and excludes leases. Borrowing/equity requires positive book equity. Current assets versus current liabilities does not establish payment ability. Cash versus borrowing does not assume cash is unrestricted. Cash flow versus borrowing is a dated flow/stock relationship, not a repayment schedule. A lower borrowing/equity ratio does not prove repayment. No earnings-quality, credit-health or investment rating is invented.

Line charts use one shared signed dollar scale including zero, actual fiscal dates and straight segments. Missing rows, a year gap and changes in concept/result definition break the path; three bridge inputs do not alone create a definition change. Legend toggles, the keyboard date slider, selected values and exact evidence remain available. Short-/long-term pairs share one zero-based scale. Positive balance-sheet blocks must reconcile to assets; each rectangle’s area is proportional to its amount. Missing categories remain in explicitly unclassified residuals, never silently zero. Negative equity or unreconciled parts suppress the positive-area breakdown while raw figures remain available. The complete labelled legend supports small blocks and phone layouts.

The page loads saved history only while selected, retains date/basis choices across navigation and fences late reads. An updated workspace payload cannot display a previous payload’s series. Source withdrawal hides the data. Evidence dialogs show exact inputs, concepts, dates, accession and original filing links; rounded display values do not replace evidence. Phones scroll charts within the page, and native keyboard/focus/reduced-motion behaviour remains.
