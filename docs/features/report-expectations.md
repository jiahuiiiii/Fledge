# Expected reporting evidence

In a numerical condition, expand **When you expect the next figures**. Choose the earliest required reporting-period end and the date you expect the figures by. Both dates are optional together. Review and approve the complete idea revision; changing either date resets approval.

Your date includes the entire UTC day. Until then, the existing compatible figures are evaluated under your other conditions. After that day, missing required figures make this condition **unknown**, retaining its last value and source. A matching or later period must use the same annual/quarterly scope, metric, unit and reporting basis. New evidence restores the ordinary threshold check, which may still fail. A same-period restatement does not provide a newer period. Conflicts and reporting-age limits remain separate.

The date is your research assumption. It is not a verified company date, a legal deadline or proof of a late filing. The app may lack newer evidence because acquisition is manual, coverage is stale, a source failed or the selected metric is unavailable. Use existing **Refresh filings** or optional daily filing checks to acquire data. Saving dates or passing a deadline makes no supplier or AI call and enables no watch.

The local worker records one meaningful deadline-state change, preserves catch-up chronology and shows it in **Updates**. History and downloads keep the exact dates, source cutoff and result. Clearing an expectation creates a new revision; old results remain. Current source permissions still apply when opening or exporting historical evidence.

This currently covers the two numerical monitoring metrics and the active selected reporting scope. It is not a verified earnings calendar, automatic recurring filing schedule or standalone filing-receipt tracker. A later matching period can satisfy the on-or-after requirement; the app does not prove that every intervening report was collected.
