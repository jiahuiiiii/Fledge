# Competitor position

The complete comparison and editable peer group live only in **Compare & value**. Financials links there with one fiscal-year growth sentence using the same compatible-peer rules. Outlook opens with management guidance and analyst forecasts, without repeating this block. Broadcom defaults to NVIDIA, AMD, Marvell, Micron and Qualcomm, as requested by the owner. Saved private peer choices override this default. Other companies use their saved choices; no sector universe or competitors are inferred from a vendor classification.

Apply previews saved figures without a private write. Save peers for this company explicitly uses the existing owner-scoped peer records and retains the displayed, editable comparison reasons. Up to eight different peers are supported. Company-directory search can add supported saved US listings. Same-issuer share classes, duplicate symbols and unknown identities are rejected. Clearing saved peers restores the default group. No peer choice is saved automatically.

`competitor-position-1` is a read-only, consistent projection over existing permission-checked SEC performance, FMP estimates, public forecasts and original management guidance. Merely opening a tab, selecting a measure, applying a group or searching the saved directory makes no provider or AI call. Check reported data for these companies explicitly invokes the existing registration and SEC refresh routes, sequentially, preserving shared pacing, leases and cooldowns. Navigation/unmount stops subsequent dispatches; an already dispatched source request retains its original outcome. No paid AI action is added.

The growth/profitability map plots saved annual revenue growth against reported GAAP operating margin. Both must share the company's exact annual window. Following the owner's clarified reference, the individual comparison uses compact **horizontal rows on one shared axis**, with a continuous peer-average marker. The researched company is highlighted; company order stays stable, negative bars extend left of zero, zero has a baseline marker and missing figures have no bar. Source methods, input identities, units, filing accessions, annual duration and period membership are checked. Unknown figures stay unknown. Amendments lacking compatible facts do not silently fall back to an original filing. **Evidence & periods** opens one modal containing every company's value and dates. Selecting a row opens that same modal with its company's exact figures, formulas, inputs, missing-value reason and original filing links expanded. Forecast comparisons retain the same shared-chart presentation with their original forecast-specific evidence.

**Peer average** is the equal-weight arithmetic mean of eligible peers, excluding the researched company and unavailable values. Annual reported/forecast figures retain the existing120-day fiscal-end comparison rule. A missing company end date prevents an annual peer average. Negative and zero reported figures remain valid. The modal lists included and excluded peers and uses original unrounded values. It is a selected-group average, never a sector universe or investment score.

**Price / earnings (P/E)** is an additional selector measure using already-saved Finnhub TTM references from the existing read-only valuation context. Only a permitted reference matching the member's symbol is used, with the existing positive-multiple validation; missing values do not fall back to FMP or a calculation from current prices. Exact decimals, source field `peTTM`, snapshot identity and observation date stay inspectable. The P/E mean uses positive usable references for selected peers from that single provider, with differing saved dates disclosed. Underlying quote times and GAAP/adjusted conventions remain unknown; this is not forward P/E. A failed reference read preserves the financial comparison and shows P/E unavailable with its read error. Reading or changing the selector makes no source/model request or private write.

The reading names which usable peers are above, below or equal to the researched company's figure. The peer median excludes the researched company and is limited to peers whose annual end is within 120 days of its end. Coverage is explicit; percentage figures use percentage-point differences. The 120-day window is a presentation rule, not proof of calendar equivalence or economic comparability. Charts retain each company's actual dates. These are whole-company observations, including acquisitions, business mix and fiscal-week differences; no investment score or sector-wide average is invented.

Compare & value keeps expected growth separate from reported position. Supported expected revenue growth uses two consecutive annual forecast averages in the same FMP response with explicit USD currencies and an ordered annual gap of 350–380 days. The preceding forecast average must be positive. It is forecast-to-forecast growth, not actual-to-forecast growth or an actual-results verdict. Fiscal ends, source identity and observation date remain inspectable. Public tables with unspecified currency cannot supply a shared revenue scale or substitute for missing FMP forecasts. Underlying forecast vintage remains unknown.

Current original management operating-margin targets retain their own quarterly end and basis. Expired, historical, withdrawn or truncated sections cannot populate current targets. Adjusted targets do not create a peer margin ranking, because company exclusions differ. Original guidance and analyst forecasts are expanded in Outlook; result comparisons, release history, evidence and provider controls remain available there. Existing paid news-expectation reading remains separate and explicit.

Phone charts scroll within their own frame; the page does not overflow. Native text labels, keyboard peer/measure selection, Evidence Escape/focus return and the application's reduced-motion policy remain supported.
