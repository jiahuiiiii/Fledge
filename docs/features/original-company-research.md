# Original company documents and business research

Implemented 9 October 2026. This adds original filing collection, a sourced beginner business brief, deeper code-calculated financial measures and FMP data access. It does not validate investment predictions, sentiment accuracy, alert usefulness or participant demand.

## Original documents

Migration 042 adds immutable original HTML/text versions, current pointers and fenced collection attempts. The collector reads canonical SEC archive URLs from retained issuer submissions: the latest reporting-period annual report, latest quarter and recent results-of-operations 8-K. It can follow up to two explicit Exhibit 99 HTML links within the same filing directory. Older-period amendments cannot outrank newer reporting periods.

The identifying header and shared SEC pacing remain. There is a 15-minute company retry interval, a three-minute collection lease and bounded 20 MiB HTML / two-million-character rendered text. No redirects, alternate host, credentials, challenge handling or automatic retry. Archive 401/403 denials persist as their own capability; a 429 honors at least 15 minutes or longer Retry-After. Structured SEC access retains its independent history.

Original bytes and content hash are retained. Rendered text removes executable/hidden elements, preserves table cells and supplies exact line offsets and passage IDs. Section detection is best effort; unsupported/absent sections remain gaps. Inline issuer IDs, when present, must match. HTML that is unreadable, challenged, too small or belongs to another issuer fails. This is a bounded HTML reader, not universal PDF/foreign-filing coverage.

Original-document timestamps mean SEC filing acceptance, including an exhibit's parent filing acceptance; they do not independently verify earnings-announcement publication. UI labels explicitly show that meaning and UTC. The structured financial reports separately retain official filing dates. These dates can differ across time zones and are not silently substituted.

One completed source group can remain available after a later failure. Versions are immutable, including first availability. Exact A→B→A content restores the original version. Permission changes hide current/historical documents and derived brief bodies. First-open source loading adds a disclosure step after structured filings; collecting original documents itself makes no AI request.

## Business brief

The **Business** tab explains operations, revenue model, customers, reported drivers, competition, financial position and risks. Prompt `thesis-business-brief-1` reads only selected whole original passages, with explicit company-statement versus interpretation labels. It selects up to 36 passages / 28,000 serialized bytes across topic terms; this is not an exhaustive reading. Company claims remain attributed and dated. Vendor profile descriptions are not substituted as original evidence.

Generation is explicit and uses the existing priced reasoning profile and cumulative ledger. Source IDs, selected passages, method and original dates determine reuse; merely reading or reopening a saved brief causes no charge. It includes no private reasoning, watch settings or implicit approval of an investment idea. Suggested questions open an editable draft only after selection.

Every citation must name an actual selected source/passage; quotations come from code's original evidence, not model-generated quotes. Validation policy `business-evidence-filter-1` withholds individual claims adding a number/period absent from their own evidence; it never rewrites them or asks the model to fix them automatically. Invalid citation/schema responses fail. Exact reference matching and numeric checks do not prove semantic support. Rejected original responses remain in the ledger/evaluation evidence.

Migration 043 retains immutable brief history. History, source inspection and inert HTML export retain dates, attribution, exact quotes, limitations and withheld-claim explanations. Permission changes withhold the saved body. Earlier policy identities stay visible rather than silently rewriting historical prose.

## Financial depth

Current display values also use the [general amendment resolver](competitor-position.md#reviewed-annual-amendments). Original snapshots, model evidence and persisted valuation bases remain unchanged; confirmed inherited inputs retain their original filing and supporting amendment evidence.

`sec-financial-depth-1` uses retained whole-company US-GAAP USD facts. Existing monitoring and performance methods are unchanged. New views include annual revenue trends, trailing revenue/operating income/net income/cash flow/capital spending, calculated free cash flow and operating margin, and borrowing.

A quarter's trailing value uses identified prior annual + current YTD − comparable prior YTD, with compatible fiscal dates and the same financial concept. Missing/conflicting values, incompatible fiscal years and unsupported units remain unknown. Every input has its exact filing, concept, period and value. Restatement/accounting-policy consistency still needs review of notes. A 52/53-week fiscal year is not converted into a calendar estimate.

Borrowing prefers a reported standard combined-debt fact; otherwise it requires compatible complete long-/short-term components. Reported total long-term debt already includes its current portion. Commercial paper is not added again to short-term borrowings. Missing components are not zero; liabilities and lease obligations remain separate. General custom-dimensional and IFRS normalization remain unimplemented; the bounded reported-revenue view below supports specific standard dimensions.

The fundamentals overview shows four dated figures, selectable reported annual revenue bars and a cash-generation comparison. All bar geometry uses a shared scale within its chart, includes zero and preserves negative values. Unavailable annual values have an explicit gap; incompatible cash-flow periods are excluded. Revenue is not joined to a trailing-year value as though they were the same report. Selecting a year or inspecting a figure makes no source or AI request. Each figure exposes exact inputs/formula/filing links. Selection transitions respect reduced motion; charts and evidence controls work at phone widths. The design is informed by the owner's Simply Wall St report reference without importing its scores, fair-value estimates or financial data.

The **What it owns and owes** section visualizes the saved balance-sheet values. Assets/liabilities share one chart scale; cash/borrowing share another. The two pairs overlap and cannot be added together. Descriptions distinguish accounting resources, all liabilities, cash equivalents and the existing complete borrowing measure. Reporting-date selection is local. Every plotted row and input must match the selected filing accession, balance date and USD unit, with no duration. An unavailable or differently dated borrowing total stays unknown; it is not replaced by a debt component or another period. Existing raw decimal strings and source links remain inspectable, and known zero balances remain zero. This changes presentation only: no new calculation method, data storage, monitoring rule, source request or paid stage. [Financial-position verification](../reviews/2026-10-09/financial-position-and-forecast-access.md).

## Reported revenue breakdown

The [revenue and expense flow](revenue-expense-flow.md) joins compatible statement totals and original categories in Financials. The [financial reading](financial-reading.md) adds retained history and dated explanations. Its versioned calculation and period rules are separate from the category reader below.

`sec-segment-revenue-1` reads retained original Inline XBRL, because the SEC companyfacts API covers whole-entity facts rather than these dimensional disclosures. **Where revenue comes from** in Fundamentals separates operating segments, products/services and reported geographies, with annual, fiscal-year-to-date and quarter periods. It makes no source or AI request and changes no stored filing, business brief, financial method, private research or watch rule. No schema migration is required.

This is a bounded reader, not a general XBRL processor. Supported inputs are well-formed Inline XBRL 1.1, recognized US-GAAP revenue concepts, simple USD units and one standard business/product/geography axis. It verifies the issuer, reporting-period context, namespaces, fact scale/sign/precision, duplicate identities and matching consolidated denominator. Unsupported transformations, typed/nested dimensions, conflicting/nil facts, custom revenue concepts and unavailable totals remain gaps. XML declarations of entities/DTDs, excessive depth/size and namespace rebinding are rejected. Source permission is checked before reading or reusing derived results.

Percentages and bars require every retained category to be usable, nonnegative and to sum **exactly** to the positive consolidated total for the same concept and period. Otherwise the individual reported amounts remain visible without a percentage chart. Geography can contain overlapping countries and regions: no hierarchy is inferred and no additive completeness is claimed from tag names. Shipping destinations, customer location and economic exposure are not interchangeable. The view links to the original filing for definitions and the attribution basis.

Labels expand filing member tags, with explicitly recognized country codes; they are not AI interpretations or standardized peer categories. Exact decimal strings, displayed source values, context/concept IDs and fact anchors remain inspectable, along with the reporting dates and SEC acceptance time. Compact amounts and percentages are rounded only for display. The category/period selectors are local, preserve keyboard access and use reduced-motion-aware transitions. An empty/unsupported filing displays its coverage gap. [Verification and current limits](../reviews/2026-10-09/reported-revenue-breakdown.md).

## Management outlook from original releases

`sec-management-outlook-1` adds a read-only **Management outlook** section in Fundamentals. It reads immutable saved earnings-release passages, with the original source ID, offsets, accession, first-saved time and SEC acceptance time. An explicit release dateline, when present, is shown separately from SEC acceptance; forecast-period dates require explicit wording in the outlook section. Current exhibits must match the current parent results filing. Up to 20 retained release versions can be selected locally; old versions stay labelled historical.

The reader recognizes bounded prose outlook sections, not arbitrary tables or a universal financial-report format. It preserves the surrounding outlook/disclaimer and original text. Only a narrow grammar types whole-company revenue points/ranges and operating income as a share of revenue. Segment wording, complex or reversed ranges and unsupported statements remain original excerpts. A dollar sign alone is not USD; an unlabeled revenue statement is not assumed GAAP. Conflicting periods, withdrawal/earlier-guidance wording and truncated context prevent automated comparison.

Automated comparison supports **explicit GAAP USD whole-company revenue** only. Guidance must have been both SEC-accepted and first saved before its explicit target period ended. The result must have the same quarter/annual type and end date, a supported duration, one compatible standard reported revenue input and the exact original filing accession. Missing inputs stay unknown. The query first selects the earliest retained non-amended filing/snapshot for each period, then limits to 100 periods; a later retained correction cannot silently replace that original result. A range comparison says above/within/below the displayed range; a point comparison shows a decimal-safe difference, retaining “approximate” and avoiding an invented pass/fail tolerance.

The displayed comparison is today's read-only projection over identified immutable source versions, not a newly saved assessment or point-in-time replay. Additional earlier evidence can alter the available projection; no old business brief or assessment is rewritten. The filing result may follow the earnings announcement and is not claimed as the earliest announcement. Original-release and structured-fact permissions are checked independently. No model, source request, migration, private condition, watch or alert is created by this view. General guidance normalization, EPS/non-GAAP/segment comparisons and independently verified real forecast-versus-result outcomes remain gaps.

The interface has a dated timeline, readable amount cards, original context, local history selection, keyboard controls and reduced-motion support. It loads when first opened. Actual saved Broadcom supplies a 2 September 2026 release dateline, a quarter ending 1 November 2026, approximately $34.8bn revenue (currency/accounting basis left unspecified) and approximately 66% non-GAAP operating income as a share of revenue. It has no eligible actual-results comparison. See [verification and retained failures](../reviews/2026-10-09/management-outlook.md).

## FMP and reviewed comparisons

Migration 045 adds separate permissions for profile, suggested peers, consensus and trailing ratios; immutable response vintages/current pointers; and owner-isolated peer selections. The key is `FMP_API_KEY`. Checking data makes no AI call. Local controls are one hour per company/dataset, at least two seconds between request reservations and 40 requests/day. Leases fence replaced/expired results. Access restrictions persist; 429 pauses global dispatch. Credentials, provider URLs carrying keys and raw error text are never surfaced to the user. There is no paid-plan purchase or restricted-endpoint workaround.

FMP peers are provider suggestions based on exchange, sector and size. Choose up to three comparisons and write why they help; product competition and valuation comparability require review. Saving does not enroll watches or collect data. Vendor P/E and P/S are explicitly TTM, positive usable values only, and remain distinct from forward estimates and SEC calculations. Existing permitted Finnhub reference values are shown in their own section, with their own retrieval dates and definitions. No automatic cheapness or investment ranking is made.

**Peer comparison** visualizes the selected company and the user's saved peers in the same order. The figure/source selector offers separate Finnhub and FMP TTM P/E/P/S, plus existing SEC trailing revenue, operating margin, free cash flow and period-end borrowing. A chart uses one provider/measure and one zero-inclusive scale; negative cash flow and known zero remain visible. Missing or withdrawn values stay unavailable, with no other-vendor fallback, incomplete-debt substitution or peer average. Exact values, source identities, definitions, private comparison rationale, original filing inputs and fiscal/observation dates remain inspectable. Collection times are not quote times. Different fiscal calendars remain explicit, and unknown earnings conventions are not inferred.

Changing chart controls makes no source or model request. No actual peer is selected automatically. Local unsaved peer edits survive research-tab navigation, source checks and delayed read responses. The chart continues using saved choices until explicit Save; Discard restores them without writing. Company scope changes remount the editor, and a signed-out workspace removes it. The forecast/peer panel and chart load on demand; motion respects the user's reduced-motion setting. [Peer-view verification](../reviews/2026-10-09/peer-comparison.md).

**Annual revenue growth · SEC** adds the existing `sec-performance-1` annual revenue-growth calculation to that selector. It preserves the exact decimal, snapshot/payload identity and two same-filing USD inputs; it does not calculate another growth rate. Both current and prior fiscal dates are visible. This is reported growth, including acquisition and currency effects, rather than an organic-growth or forecast claim. A missing annual report, prior period, usable positive denominator or current source permission leaves it unavailable; quarterly and trailing data are never substituted. The existing calculation's supported range and missing reasons remain unchanged. Negative and zero growth stay visible on the shared scale. [Annual-growth verification](../reviews/2026-10-09/peer-revenue-growth.md).

Consensus stores annual revenue/EPS low/average/high, analyst counts and exact observed vintages. Missing currency is explicit. An estimate retrieved after a result is not a verified pre-release forecast; missing fiscal, currency or earnings conventions prevent a comparable actual-results verdict. Current response history is retained rather than backfilled with later forecasts. Automated consensus-versus-actual evaluation remains future work.

Actual Broadcom access: profile and suggested peers returned HTTP 200. Ratios and the integrated consensus check returned HTTP 402 requiring subscription access. Those restrictions remain; the UI does not invent the missing figures or claim all named FMP datasets are connected. The original first estimates probe failed before its non-JSON diagnostic was saved; that incomplete evidence remains separate from the later explicit status.

The separate [public annual forecast connection](public-financial-forecasts.md) reads Stock Analysis's limited visible revenue and adjusted-EPS tables from the same bounded page request as existing price targets. It preserves the shared 24-hour company cooldown, independent source permission and immutable observations. Restricted years remain omitted; forecast currency and pre-release comparability remain unknown. FMP restrictions are preserved. The external source link remains available for direct reading.
