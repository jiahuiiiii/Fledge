# Company reading journey

Current presentation contract, 9 October 2026. This reorganizes existing research features without changing acquisition, AI interpretation, source identity, account isolation or monitoring policies.

## Company views

Real companies start on **Overview**, followed by **Financials**, **News & discussion**, **Outlook**, and **Compare & value**. Internal tab keys stay stable. Arrow keys follow the horizontal/vertical menu orientation; Home/End select its endpoints. Fictional companies have Overview, Financials and News & discussion, with a visibly fictional overview. The company sidebar can still collapse to a logo rail.

Overview reads the saved business brief and shows existing dated financial highlights and revenue mix. Business/revenue-model/risks sections start open; other business topics remain available on expansion. Missing explanations stay missing until the user explicitly creates a brief. Financials retains detailed financial charts, original documents, filing-watch controls and source inputs. The price quote stays visible and the price chart starts collapsed.

Outlook presents original management guidance, analyst forecasts and the separately labelled news-extraction feature. Compare & value presents the existing private peer editor/charts and valuation scenarios. FMP views read existing saved context; navigation does not refresh providers. The peer editor remains one retained component, with explicit Save/Discard and protection against late reads overwriting unsaved or newly saved choices.

## Evidence and coverage

News & discussion starts with recent company headlines, with up to five stories before an explicit Show all control. Displayed snippets use a three-line preview; the saved source retains the complete supplied text. The optional AI company briefing is expandable. Sentiment analysis follows the news and starts with its company-news filter. Headlines, source dates, AI labels, a short interpretation and report-repeat/conflict notices remain on cards. **Inspect evidence** opens exact excerpts, old/current wording, report comparisons, reply context and the link to the original source. Nested source dialogs retain the original source identity and return keyboard focus to their caller.

The panel’s **Evidence** button opens the analysed sample, current saved sources and history. These scopes remain distinct: current raw texts do not borrow labels from an older analysis. Saved labels retain their original cutoff and method; changed selection is not presented as a measured sentiment change. Limits and source-window explanations remain inspectable. Source counts are bounded candidate/selection counts, not independent votes or guarantees of usefulness.

**Data & sources**, in the company header and the news panel, holds collection counts, publisher/provider checks, per-community checks and the source library. Missing source access remains a coverage gap. Social failures, stale/earlier-method analysis, unavailable news connections, changed current inputs and withheld readings remain visible near affected content. Moving diagnostics into a dialog does not make missing evidence neutral or imply every configured source returned content.

Financial highlights and financial-position measures open their exact values, formulas, dates, input concepts and filing links in an **Evidence** dialog. Existing unavailable-value and comparability restrictions are unchanged.

Business **Original evidence** shows the statement being checked above its sources. Long quotations start with a clearly labelled preview of the first 420 characters, shortened at a word boundary; **Read full passage** reveals the complete, unchanged saved quotation. This preview is not a new AI summary or a claim that the opening lines alone support the finding. Source identity, SEC acceptance and original filing links remain available. A padded scrolling body keeps the header and close control visible; reopening starts with previews collapsed.

## Private research and watches

**My research** replaces the permanent right-hand idea column. The dialog contains the question library/answer workflow, saved reasoning, latest assessment and research decision. A short question summary remains in the workspace. Closing the dialog preserves unsaved question edits; changing company unmounts that company’s form and closes dialogs. Existing late-response fencing remains. Saving reasoning opens the original editor; approvals and private revisions retain their contracts.

**Watch · on/off** opens monitoring controls and optional private-check settings. Opening, closing and reading this dialog do not write settings. Explicit checkbox/selection actions use the existing routes. Turning a watch off preserves its saved context/event/purpose choices; advanced controls remain hidden while off, while saved watch history stays available through Evidence. Existing watches are not enrolled, stopped or retargeted by this layout change.

Progress is compact by default for new browser preferences; existing progress choices persist. Active work and attention states remain visible on its control. Paid actions remain explicit and use the original installation ledger. No app or database restart is required to install this frontend change.

Dialogs use native dialog semantics, Escape dismissal, focus return, an explicit keyboard boundary and responsive scroll containment. Only My research and watch settings retain their children while closed, to preserve form state. Software/browser checks do not establish beginner usability or model accuracy; independent participant validation remains future work.
