# Company reading journey

Current presentation contract, 9 October 2026. This reorganizes existing research features without changing acquisition, AI interpretation, source identity, account isolation or monitoring policies.

## Company views

Real companies start on **Overview**, followed by **Financials**, **News & discussion**, **Outlook**, and **Compare & value**. Internal tab keys stay stable. The section menu is horizontal at every width, leaving the company list as the only left column. Left/Right arrows navigate its tabs; Home/End select its endpoints. Fictional companies have Overview, Financials and News & discussion, with a visibly fictional overview. Company pages default to the existing 64px logo rail. The one-time layout-default version preserves the user’s subsequent expand/collapse choice. Names, browsing and the Your research card remain available when expanded.

Overview follows the owner’s five-section reference: **1 How the company makes money**, **2 Growth & outlook**, **3 Financial health**, **4 News & discussion**, **5 Value**. Each numbered section has a compact reading and status chips, then paired key-information and visual/source cards. The cards stack on phones. No company/section sidebar is added; the existing logo rail and horizontal navigation stay unchanged. The saved AI business brief is an expandable part of section1, with the original generation, citations, history and question controls retained. Missing explanations stay missing until the user explicitly creates a brief. Financials opens with a summary row and three charts: sales becoming profit, sales over time, and what the company owns/owes. **More detail** holds segments, liquidity, borrowing history, composition, raw figures and filings. Underlined figures open their evidence directly, including exact current/prior periods for growth. Competitor positioning lives only in Compare & value; Financials uses a short fiscal-year comparison sentence/link, with only compatible available peers in its denominator. The owner’s new Overview reference explicitly brings the existing revenue-flow chart into section1 too; both views retain their independent period choices and the same data/evidence. A single available trailing period does not get a redundant year selector in Overview. The quote stays visible on company pages and the price chart starts collapsed.

Outlook opens with company guidance: larger figure cards, the release’s stated date and forecast-period end, plus an Evidence dialog containing the exact original passage, raw value, SEC acceptance, first saved time and release link. Historical releases, result comparisons and their missing-input reasons remain available. Context, release metadata and coverage rules start collapsed. Analyst forecasts follow in separate FMP and Stock Analysis/S&P Global sections; revenue and earnings cards show low/average/high values on an independently scaled range. Negative and zero forecasts remain valid, and missing endpoints have no plotted range. Currency and earnings conventions stay explicit; exact values, immutable observations and source limitations are expandable. The public source’s next permitted check is visible beside its disabled button. Expectations reported in the news start collapsed, retain their saved reading and expansion on tab navigation, and keep extraction as an explicit AI action. Compare & value presents the existing private peer editor/charts and valuation scenarios. FMP views read existing saved context; navigation does not refresh providers. The peer editor remains one retained component, with explicit Save/Discard and protection against late reads overwriting unsaved or newly saved choices.

## Evidence and coverage

News & discussion starts with recent company headlines, with up to five stories before an explicit Show all control. Displayed snippets use a three-line preview; the saved source retains the complete supplied text. The optional AI company briefing is expandable. Sentiment analysis follows the news and starts with its company-news filter. Headlines, source dates, AI labels, a short interpretation and report-repeat/conflict notices remain on cards. **Inspect evidence** opens exact excerpts, old/current wording, report comparisons, reply context and the link to the original source. Nested source dialogs retain the original source identity and return keyboard focus to their caller.

The panel’s **Evidence** button opens the analysed sample, current saved sources and history. These scopes remain distinct: current raw texts do not borrow labels from an older analysis. Saved labels retain their original cutoff and method; changed selection is not presented as a measured sentiment change. Limits and source-window explanations remain inspectable. Source counts are bounded candidate/selection counts, not independent votes or guarantees of usefulness.

**Data & sources**, in the company header and the news panel, holds collection counts, publisher/provider checks, per-community checks and the source library. Missing source access remains a coverage gap. Social failures, stale/earlier-method analysis, unavailable news connections, changed current inputs and withheld readings remain visible near affected content. Moving diagnostics into a dialog does not make missing evidence neutral or imply every configured source returned content.

Financial highlights and financial-position measures open their exact values, formulas, dates, input concepts and filing links in an **Evidence** dialog. Existing unavailable-value and comparability restrictions are unchanged.

Business **Original evidence** shows the statement being checked above its sources. Long quotations start with a clearly labelled preview of the first 420 characters, shortened at a word boundary; **Read full passage** reveals the complete, unchanged saved quotation. This preview is not a new AI summary or a claim that the opening lines alone support the finding. Source identity, SEC acceptance and original filing links remain available. A padded scrolling body keeps the header and close control visible; reopening starts with previews collapsed.

## Private research and watches

**Ask a question** is the consistently named action opening **Your research**. It appears in the company header and the existing left-column card when that sidebar is expanded; there is no separate top-bar research button, Notebook label or second left research column. The optional starter guide lives inside this same dialog. Questions explore a company; the idea is the user's saved point of view. Original question library/answers, saved reasoning, assessments and decision controls remain. Closing preserves question edits; changing company closes and resets its form. Saving opens the original editor, with unchanged approval and revision controls.

**Watch · on/off** opens monitoring controls and optional private-check settings. Opening, closing and reading this dialog do not write settings. Explicit checkbox/selection actions use the existing routes. Turning a watch off preserves its saved context/event/purpose choices; advanced controls remain hidden while off, while saved watch history stays available through Evidence. Existing watches are not enrolled, stopped or retargeted by this layout change.

The company header's **Research updates** control opens saved report metadata, **Refresh research**, and the original animated progress/details controls. Existing progress-collapse preferences persist. A small activity dot marks an active research run; the report itself has no permanent filing/progress strip. Fictional recorded companies retain their explicit fictional-demo strip and scenario action. Phone headers use labelled 44px icon controls and a compact identity/quote layout; source, timestamp and supported price change remain visible. An alternate quote-supplier error or interrupted-check diagnostic lives in **Data & sources** rather than beneath an otherwise valid compact quote.

AI availability appears beside affected actions. An unresolved earlier request still blocks new paid actions with plain-language paused guidance; the original charge explanation is available under collapsed **AI request details** in Research updates. There is no global billing banner, automatic charge review, retry or hold release. Paid actions remain explicit and use the original installation ledger. No app or database restart is required to install this frontend change. See [content-first layout verification](../reviews/2026-10-09/content-first-ui.md).

Dialogs use native dialog semantics, Escape dismissal, focus return, an explicit keyboard boundary and responsive scroll containment. Only Your research and watch settings retain their children while closed, to preserve form state. Software/browser checks do not establish beginner usability or model accuracy; independent participant validation remains future work.

## My ideas

My ideas leads with the selected company scope and saved-idea count. The empty state offers **Save my reasoning** for a selected company, opening the existing editor, plus **Read company research** to return to that company’s Workspace. With All companies selected, **Choose a company** opens the existing company browser. Opening or cancelling these forms saves nothing and does not enroll monitoring.

Saved cards keep the existing company filter and descending unread-count order. Each shows the company avatar/name/ticker, literal saved status, original question, a three-line reasoning preview, unchanged awaiting-review count, evidence cutoff and revision. The full original reasoning remains on the existing idea page/editor. Long company names ellipsize with their full title available; missing logos use the existing fallback. The whole card is a keyboard-accessible button to the existing idea page. Colours, typography and corners use shared tokens; phone layouts, focus and reduced motion remain supported.

## Collection pages and Updates

My ideas contains saved points of view. Each idea page contains a **History · versions and evidence checks** disclosure; the top-level History tab is removed. Old company/history links and exact evaluation/revision links open that idea with History expanded. History contains earlier saved versions and completed checks. An empty History has one explanation and a **Go to My ideas** action; an empty source-check section is omitted. Existing nonempty checks remain under **News checked against your idea**, with original evidence and review controls. All-company and selected-company histories retain their original scopes and record links.

Ideas, History and Updates use the same reading width and gutters as company pages. Their unrelated company quote header is hidden. Updates uses its own company/status filters, so its mobile company picker is also hidden. **Weekly review** appears once, as an Updates heading action with its existing unseen count; the duplicate banners are removed. Filters start in an explicit disclosure showing the current company/status; source coverage follows the records. Original paging, cutoff, counts, review writes and delayed-result protection remain.

Company update previews use the alert's first changed source: its exact retained headline and own cited body excerpt, or its own body if no compatible excerpt exists. The source name/date and original alert category remain secondary. Never borrow another source, a comparison-only article, a parent comment or a later workspace version. The saved generic AI explanation stays in the expanded evidence. Withheld records expose none of this content. No new model summary, stored-title rewrite or financial claim is introduced. See [reading-flow verification](../reviews/2026-10-09/reading-flow.md).

## News and discussion rows

Saved headlines and original source text lead each row. The known social placeholders **Hacker News comment**, **Reddit comment**, **Reddit post**, and **X post** are source types, so they do not become large headings in the feed or its saved/current source views. Real titles remain exact, including any publisher headline with the same words. Never substitute a parent headline or parent author’s words for a comment’s own text.

The feed shows an exact four-line preview of the existing permitted `source.body`, with smaller platform/date metadata and secondary **AI label**, tone and statement type. The saved generic classification explanation lives in the existing Story evidence dialog, beside the full original text, original citations, parent context and comparison controls. Full saved/current source bodies remain unchanged. Previews may include raw fragments; the existing label still refers to its retained assessed passages. No generated summary, new source/model request, label rewrite or inferred author is introduced. Original text remains plain escaped text. Pagination, ordering, relevance/platform filters, separate tone samples, withdrawal/withholding, source inspection, watches and paid actions retain their existing rules.

## Company identity and All companies snapshots

Known company display names use one small presentation map, including Advanced Micro Devices, Qualcomm, Marvell Technology, Micron Technology and Broadcom. The canonical source/legal names remain unchanged and available as titles; company search matches both names and the ticker. Unknown companies and fictional recorded companies keep their original names. Header, sidebar, mobile picker, company manager, saved ideas and All companies use this same presentation rule.

The unmodified bundled Marvell mark is white on transparent, so its avatar has a dark token-based frame. Other existing logo handling, unavailable-image initials, recorded-company behavior and CSP remain unchanged. The asset source and hash are retained in [company logo sources](../../frontend/src/assets/company-logos/SOURCES.md).

All companies presents a responsive card grid with company identity, saved price and its source/timestamp, price change where supported, SEC trailing revenue and operating margin with period ends, and the existing idea/unread state. It uses existing account-scoped workspace and price-history GETs, at most three companies concurrently, reusing the initial matching workspace. Browsing and **Reload saved data** never refresh a provider or generate a reading. No figure is estimated for missing compatible inputs. Cards remain keyboard-accessible links to the existing workspace; search includes canonical legal names. Membership changes/unmounts fence pending responses; explicit reload and read failures remain visible. News tone and its saved date are also present, with missing/withheld readings left unavailable. These remain separate from the dated price and financial periods.

The header and cards share the same quote/change presentation. When a newer permitted Yahoo quote is selected, its change uses only a completed close in the **same saved series**, with no omitted sessions and a supported session label. Regular/pre-market quotes compare against the latest completed bar strictly before the New York session date; after-hours may use that date's completed close. The UI explicitly says **vs [date] close**, without assuming an exchange calendar or claiming an old saved quote is today's price. Missing/provisional/invalid/zero baselines withhold the change; a Yahoo price never borrows Finnhub's delta. When Finnhub supplies the chosen price, use its own saved change and independently known percentage. Zero and negative changes remain valid. Source clocks, history refresh controls, access withdrawal and background policies retain their existing contracts.

## News counts and section status

The Overview news card and News reading distinguish selected stories, relevant originals and grouped developments. The saved Broadcom example is **65 stories → 30 relevant → 20 distinct developments**, with 8 positive, 8 neutral/mixed and 4 negative. Unclear groups remain a visible category and count in the total. Repeated coverage counts once; this is the original bounded AI grouping, not independently verified developments or investor consensus. Earlier non-development methods retain a text-group label. No saved count or classification is rewritten.

One **Details** line combines the news-feed failure count/denominator and latest checked time, discussion feed failures, incomplete watch and older/method flags. Exact provider messages, watch error, paid-request detail and Data & sources next step remain inside. News includes Finnhub once when its saved status is present; social connections are not added to the news denominator. A disabled **Refresh & analyse** button has a programmatically linked reason beside it. Discussion window, watch controls and saved-reading metadata remain in the collapsed **Reading settings** row. Withholding stays explicit at the result. Overview's incomplete-watch message links to News for feed status and next steps. No retry, charge review, hold release or source policy changes.
