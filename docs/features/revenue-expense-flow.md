# Revenue and expense flow

Implemented 9 October 2026 in Financials, inspired by the owner's reference. The flow moved into the Financials reading on the same day; Overview keeps the compact company summary. `sec-income-flow-1` reads retained SEC facts without new data/model requests or changes to monitoring, snapshots or previous financial methods.

Up to five annual and five directly reported quarterly periods are available, plus the latest compatible trailing year using the existing annual + current YTD − prior comparable YTD bridge. Fiscal dates and filing vintages stay explicit; later amendments replace that period's display choice, including a gap if they omit figures.

The diagram follows revenue → gross profit → operating profit → net result, with costs branching out. Connected USD figures share exact dates and reconcile using decimal arithmetic. Missing gross profit, cost of revenue or operating expenses may be explicitly calculated; contradictory reported figures are not replaced. Losses, non-positive revenue, negative costs and unreconciled core figures show signed values without positive-width flows. Net losses or net gains beyond operating profit end the flow at operating profit, preserving available net figures below.

`ProfitLoss` includes non-controlling interests; `NetIncomeLoss` is the parent's result. The selected definition is explicit. **Tax & other net items** is the operating-to-net difference, not a separately reported expense. It may include tax, interest, gains/losses, discontinued operations and non-controlling interests. Operating-expense detail uses reported R&D, combined selling/general/administrative and a labelled calculated remainder. Missing categories are not zero and separate sales/admin costs are never added again to a combined total.

Revenue categories join only when the original-filing reader supplies a reconciled mix matching exact dates, filing URL, concept and total. A single-filing mix never joins a trailing bridge. Dimensions remain separate. Supported category sets connect to revenue; larger sets remain in the complete breakdown. Overlapping geography values are not pooled.

Bands share one scale. Clickable figures show exact inputs, formulas, dates and filing links. Full figures, expenses and definitions are expandable. Phone diagrams scroll inside their container and have an accessible figure list. Keyboard focus, permission checks and reduced motion remain. No chart schema migration. See [verification](../reviews/2026-10-09/revenue-expense-flow.md).

## Historical revenue sources and bounded chart — 10 October 2026

Overview and Financials pass the same revenue breakdown data to their shared flow. The Revenue sources control is always present: total revenue, business segments, products/services, and countries/regions. The chosen dimension persists across annual/quarterly dates; missing dimensions explain the gap without substituting another period. Reported categories that overlap or fail reconciliation remain readable as individual figures with source dialogs, never percentage flows. Historical absence is distinguished from an already retained report with no supported breakdown.

Normal original-filing checks now also retain the flow's five annual and five quarterly periods, including bounded same-period amendment chains. Historical documents never replace `disclosure_current` or become current business/guidance material. Previously retained originals are reused after hash verification. Existing entitlement, 15-minute wait, SEC pacing/denial and fenced leases apply; a failed request stops collection without retry. The operator-only history pass uses the same path and preserves current coverage. Opening a page makes no collection request.

`sec-segment-revenue-2` reads exact retained original URLs selected by income-flow inputs, including an earlier original only when the existing amendment resolver has authorized its figures. It selects the same reported revenue concept, exact period and total as the selected flow. Older `dei/2021q4` date namespaces are supported. A standard `ConsolidationItemsAxis=OperatingSegmentsMember` qualifier is supported only beside a standard business-segment axis; original dimensions remain on evidence. Corporate, elimination, custom and other multidimensional contexts remain excluded. Conflicts and unreconciled sums remain unavailable for proportional charts. This qualifier follows the [FASB segment reporting guide](https://www.fasb.org/segmentreporting_1); it does not imply that intersegment revenue is consolidated revenue.

Comparatives are read separately for an optional past-12-month breakdown. The bridge must use the exact annual/current-YTD/prior-YTD revenue inputs, explicit contiguous dates, the same concept, axis and identical member/label sets, three individually reconciled nonnegative mixes, and a nonnegative derived mix summing exactly to the trailing total. Each category retains all original figure links and the calculation. Category changes or overlapping geography do not receive an inferred mix. UI joins validate the complete input basis, and evidence closes when the supplied data/source coverage changes.

The SVG uses measured CSS-pixel width, fixed-size text and a height below 480px; simple charts cannot magnify to fill a wide screen. The connected layout below supports up to six categories; larger sets use the compact figure list above the total-to-profit flow. Small screens scroll the diagram inside its own container. Every listed category remains keyboard-accessible with the shared readable evidence dialog.

## Connected country and regional views — 10 October 2026

The follow-up screenshot exposed two separate full geographic disclosures merged by their common XBRL axis. `sec-segment-revenue-3` separates source-backed views instead of treating countries and regions as additive parts of one total. A regional table must contain its own same-period/concept consolidated total; a bounded prose paragraph must explicitly describe revenue by country. Each whole disclosed set must reconcile exactly, sets must be disjoint and together cover every original member, and document-wide missing/conflicting/negative-value checks still apply. No subset-sum search, inferred geographic hierarchy, country-to-region allocation, or issuer override is used.

A country paragraph may cross a page only through its explicit Inline XBRL continuation link: the last block of one continuation, first block of the next, an unfinished sentence with lowercase continuation, a four-link bound and a 6,000-character limit. Missing, cyclic or unsupported continuations never supply a completed partition. Original facts, values, IDs, dimensions, dates and links remain intact. View identity and labels also fence trailing bridges, so a country view cannot join regional inputs.

Countries & regions offers separate geographic views where supported. Broadcom's five saved annual periods now have distinct country and regional flows; its quarterly reports supply regions. Reconciled sets of up to six categories connect directly into total revenue, with side labels, a common thickness scale and chart height below480px. Unsupported or larger sets remain explicit figures without fabricated connections. Duplicate source cards move into View all figures & evidence when the graph already contains them. The standalone detailed breakdown uses the same view distinction, and Overview/Financials share controls and evidence.

## Compact period controls — 10 October 2026

The shared Overview/Financials flow calls the trailing window **Past 12 months**, with a nearby explanation that it ends on the latest financial report. Annual means one complete company financial year, which can differ from the calendar year; Quarterly uses the original quarter-ending date. These labels do not change the saved period bounds or arithmetic.

Period types use quiet text tabs with an active underline. Annual years and quarterly dates use compact, horizontally scrollable buttons instead of repeating revenue in large cards. The selected period's exact dates, revenue figure and original evidence remain in the chart. A single available period does not add a redundant date picker. Native pressed states, 44px targets, focus visibility and automatic selected-date visibility remain; source and geographic selections continue to apply only to matching original data.
