## What was evaluated

Two instruction changes were tested together, with the same GPT-5.4 medium/9,000 profile, source packets, schema, citation validation and original criteria:

1. Separate a clear question with no opinion (neutral) from an ambiguous target or meaning (unclear). Assertions of praise, criticism or conditional benefit inside questions retain their expressed direction. Parent opinions cannot become a child's stance.
2. Select current wording and explicitly superseded earlier wording into their own lists, with an exception for earlier context necessary to resolve the target or current reference.

No new model/profile, output field, checker or source service was added. The v10 instruction's old parent rule conflicts with neutral intelligible questions; the candidate resolves that conflict in its prompt, but that does not by itself establish correct outputs or authorize ignoring the rest of the gate.

### Concrete comparison-prose finding

The Amazon repeat explanation says both reports concern a Constellation agreement for a Maryland plant. The later selected headline only states a twenty-year nuclear deal; the counterparty and location are named in the reference report. Exact quote validation does not catch this transfer of details. The raw explanation is preserved in `coverage-review.json` and was not published. This is a clearer unsupported-prose issue than resolving a subjective negative-versus-mixed label.

The next phase should address this directly: preserve the paired original quotations and comparison status while preventing new summaries from attributing details to both reports unless both actually supply them. A deterministic description of the comparison category with exact source-specific excerpts is preferable to another unverified free-prose repair layer. It must preserve old records and keep comparison decisions themselves labelled as model interpretations.
