## Findings against the intended purpose

All 35 accepted items and five news-comparison links were reviewed. The three Microsoft regressions classify both leadership-departure reports as negative and infer a negative stance from the ambiguous “AI does Microsoft” reply. The spending/margin warning remains neutral. These are disagreements with the predeclared product criteria; a label on a suggestive headline is partly interpretive, not an objectively verified investment effect. The ambiguous reply is especially unsuitable as a confident sentiment signal.

Amazon preserves all ten baseline labels, including neutral financing and counterparty reports. The authored plain question remains unclear and the descriptive patching practice remains negative. The unchanged v10 prompt itself contains tension between neutral intelligible questions and its parent-context instruction to use unclear when the child expresses no stance. More reasoning cannot be assumed to resolve an inconsistent contract; this trial deliberately did not edit that instruction.

Both accepted authored past/current cases now select the exact current and earlier passages separately; neither did in the original phase47 baseline. This baseline count is corrected by the phase58 raw-response audit. That narrower improvement does not outweigh the tone regressions and incomplete NVIDIA case. Unselected concerns remain: the Microsoft patching comment and the shared earnings-yield comparison still receive negative labels. No new claim of broad correctness, prospective alert precision/recall or participant usefulness is made.

All **88 quotation associations** match their frozen source scope and original text: 62 current, two earlier-attitude, eight parent and sixteen news-link associations. Exact quotations permit inspection; they do not prove the label or comparison explanation. News links retain their original comparison sources and do not transfer counterparties' returns to the target.

## Five-perspective review

These are five review perspectives applied by one coding agent, not independent professional consultants or user research.

| Perspective | Judgment and consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Product | The app needs evidence that helps revisit an idea. Higher effort did not improve the selected tone task; preserve the existing experience and avoid promoting a score increase that did not occur. |
| Research quality | Preserve the negative result, ambiguous criteria and prompt tension. Resolve the task definition before another tuning round, then use fresh held-out sources alongside the original controls. |
| UX | More than two minutes followed by no result is poor pitch behavior. Keep current defaults; retain understandable failure and original-source inspection. No new controls are justified by this experiment. |
| Architecture | The evaluation is isolated by a named bounded profile and durable shared accounting. No schema or pipeline expansion is needed. Keep the failed v11 candidate and generic checker unwired. |
| Business | Extra reasoning increased cost without an accepted quality improvement. Do not spend the remaining allowance on automatic retries or claim viable subscription margins from this sample. |
