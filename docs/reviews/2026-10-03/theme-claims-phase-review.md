## First complete claim review

The four method-4 requests completed. Reviewing all 27 generated claims found 22 supported by their selected quotations, three needing an unselected title/antecedent, one interpretive overstatement (“offset” in investor sentiment), and one current-stance omission. The NVIDIA note quoted old dislike but omitted later respect. This distinction matters: the old sentence was literally present, but the resulting note misrepresented the source's current view. No method-4 result was promoted into product research. The complete per-claim review and all original responses remain in the evidence folder.

Method 5 keeps the same structure but treats necessary contrasts as part of a coherent finding, requires the later opinion when a source explicitly changes stance, and requires title/antecedent passages when they supply a role, company identity or stock-return reference. It also avoids converting coexisting demand/cost statements into a causal offset. The original four source packets and review criteria were frozen again before explicit tests. 81 focused claim/budget checks pass; there is no automatic paid retry or source substitution.


## Title context and distinct claims

Method 5 preserved NVIDIA's current opinion and removed the causal “offset” wording. A Microsoft counterparty claim still relied on its source title to identify whose revenue was being discussed. The Amazon response was rejected because two distinct valuation findings came from one source; that rejection did not indicate mixed source ownership. The authored method-5 request was never dispatched after this structural rejection. All raw responses/charges remain retained, and no method-4/5 output was published.

Method 6 retains a complete eligible news/Reddit title as explicit per-claim context under evidence policy `theme-source-title-1`. Generic HN titles and ellipsis-bearing titles are excluded. This is deterministic source metadata, visibly labelled and distinguished from model-selected body passages; it is not an invented model quotation or a silent rewrite of old readings. Social claims still require body evidence. Multiple distinct claims may cite the same source, while exact repeated source claims are rejected and source counts deduplicate by source ID. This preserves complete meaning without requiring several facts to be compressed into one claim.

83 focused claim/budget checks pass after these corrections. Four original packets and the original review criteria were frozen for explicit method-6 evaluation; the evidence policy is part of the reviewed output contract. The previous 700-check integrated run predates this final refinement. Final integrated and actual-case evidence follows below when available.

## Method 6 and the second-pass decision

Method 6 completed all four structural renders, but semantic review still found two substantive failures: NVIDIA paired historical/possible stock growth against current GPU economics, although both can be true; Amazon changed nine non-clearing firms into Amazon plus nine others. Claim-level citations alone did not solve these errors. The source-title policy passed 702 integrated backend checks, twelve frontend checks/build and the theme browser journey; those are software checks, not a semantic pass.

The implementation now adds a separate bounded evidence-check request. It must decide on every theme and gap exactly once, with a reason. Code filters whole themes/gaps without rewriting them. An incomplete or malformed check fails publication. New saved records identify the checking call; old outputs remain unchanged. Both calls use the original cumulative ledger, and settled synthesis responses are reused. Source access is rechecked before the second paid step. Watches and sentiment labels remain unchanged.

Five-perspective follow-up: product keeps inspectable source reading when a theme is removed; research treats the checker as fallible rather than independent verification; ML/data records misses and omissions alongside catches; architecture retains both immutable calls without a second database or migration; UX discloses two paid steps and withheld themes. These are parent-authored perspectives, not independent consultants.
