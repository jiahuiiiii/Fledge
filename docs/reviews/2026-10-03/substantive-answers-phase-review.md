## What changed

`thesis-watch-relevance-9` adds an answer category, exact question target and exact source excerpt. Code rejects answer alerts classified as questions/missing information/background, missing pairs, targets outside their selected saved anchor and excerpts outside their selected original source passages. A parent-only statement cannot supply the child's answer. Both sides of the pair appear in cards, history and private/periodic downloads; the full source evidence remains inspectable. Older results are not rewritten.

A negative answer is not the same as missing data. A report saying no contracts renewed can answer whether renewals occurred. A launch announcement giving no figures cannot answer that question, but can answer whether that announcement disclosed figures. A question-shaped post may contain a real firsthand assertion; punctuation is not used as a blanket filter. Direct observations and attributed source arguments may remain background context, explicitly labelled with no alert, while inferred links still cannot trigger one.

The question-only v2 route remains unchanged: four retained requests and identities match. No migration, source acquisition, extra model stage, watch activation or private result publication occurs. The generic finding checker remains evaluation-only.
