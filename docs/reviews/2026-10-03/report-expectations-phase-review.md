# Phase 57 — expected reporting evidence

## Intended purpose and behavior

A currently acceptable figure should not remain reassuring indefinitely when the user expects a newer reporting period. Numerical conditions now optionally carry a **required period ending on or after** a chosen day and a **figures expected by** date. Both are explicitly approved in the same immutable idea revision. They are user assumptions, not company announcements, legal deadlines or inferred fiscal calendars.

Before the inclusive UTC expected-by date ends, compatible saved figures continue through the existing threshold and age checks. From midnight starting the next day, missing required figures make that condition unknown while preserving the last value and source. This applies to required and risk conditions; an old risk reading is not proof about a period for which data is missing. Other definite failures still determine the combined outcome under the existing evaluator contract.

A matching or later reporting period in the same metric, unit, basis and annual/quarterly scope resumes the ordinary threshold check. Arrival does not imply a favourable result. A same-period restatement cannot satisfy a newer-period requirement. Conflicting matching-period observations remain explicitly conflicting and unknown; a separately expired age limit can keep an available period unknown. Missing evidence in this app does not establish whether the company filed late, whether SEC has newer information or whether the supplier is current.

The existing durable cursor and job pipeline records deadline transitions even without a new document. Catch-up retains the interval of missing evidence before processing a later snapshot. It creates a grouped in-app Update, with previous/current state and the chosen dates, rather than making an extra supplier or model call. Existing explicit manual/daily filing checks remain the acquisition mechanism; this feature enables no watch. Closing the local app stops its clock work until restart/catch-up.

Dates survive editing, pending numerical suggestions, before/after review, History and individual/weekly downloads. Clearing or changing them requires a new approved revision. Source withdrawal withholds the new reporting-state metadata; a restored source can make the saved metadata visible again without rewriting the original assessment.

## Contract and compatibility

Migration 032 adds nullable `expected_period_end` and `expected_report_by` to versioned numerical definitions, plus the existing change table's `reporting` kind. Dates must be supplied together, ordered and at most 3,650 days apart. Neither a saved definition nor an old model packet is silently assigned dates. Default null fields stay out of legacy manifests and proposal inputs. Only configured expectations use evaluator `thesis-evaluator-6`; existing required-only v4 and risk v5 behavior remains unchanged. No model prompt, price, provider, schema stage or source adapter changes.

The calendar requirement is **partly** closed: user-defined numerical evidence deadlines now work. The app still has no verified supplier/company earnings calendar, inferred expected filing date, standalone filing-receipt tracker, automatic next-report schedule or general calendar screen. It monitors the two supported numerical figures in the selected active reporting scope, not every row in Financial Performance. These limits remain in the requirements matrix rather than being treated as full calendar completion.

## Five-perspective review

These are five perspectives applied by one coding agent, not independent consultants or participant research.

| Perspective | Judgment and next implication |
| --- | --- |
| Product | A user can now make “I need newer results by this date” enforceable in the saved thesis. The unknown alert asks for evidence review; an arriving report still has to pass the user's threshold. This is useful progress toward evidence freshness, while a sourced earnings calendar remains separate work. |
| Research quality | Real-record replay verifies period/scope/date mechanics and source retention. It does not verify future reporting dates, source completeness, event interpretation or investment usefulness. A missing value, conflicting value and wrong reporting scope stay distinct. |
| UX | Optional progressive disclosure avoids demanding dates from every beginner. Full-width labelled fields, current evidence periods and explicit before/after approval make the choice inspectable. Whether students understand on-or-after period matching and choose useful dates remains unmeasured. |
| Architecture | Two nullable fields extend the existing evaluator, cursor, immutable history and grouped Updates. No new collector, model pipeline or scheduler is needed. Legacy inputs/signatures and existing table values remain unchanged. |
| Business | The workflow offers another concrete reason to revisit a saved idea. Zero additional AI spending for date transitions is an engineering property, not evidence of retention, willingness to pay or total operating margins. |

Install this bounded expected-evidence capability.
