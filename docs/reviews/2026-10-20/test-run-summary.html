Alerts and Telegram

What happened

  • 11 importance alerts: GOOGL 6, NVDA 3, AVGO 2. 9 were previews (before 07:17), 2 were live.
  • 0 adverse-news alerts. No new article was judged bad for a watched company.
  • 3 price moves: META down (22:15), AVGO up (23:30), QCOM up (23:45). Two explanations found a cause; QCOM's found none.
  • Telegram: 1 alert (AVGO, 11:01, high urgency) plus 1 test message. NVDA (medium) is held for the 07:00 daily summary.

Why so few messages

  • Until 07:17 only adverse news was sent. Importance alerts and price moves were deliberately previews.
  • Price-move sending was tied to the importance-alert switch, which was not obvious. All sending was turned on at 07:17.
  • The US market is closed during Singapore daytime, so no price moves and little news until 21:30.
  • With everything on, last night would have sent 4 immediate messages and 8 in the daily summary.
