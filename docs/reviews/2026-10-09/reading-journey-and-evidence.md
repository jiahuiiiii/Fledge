# Reading journey and evidence dialogs — 9 October 2026

The owner approved the proposed company reading journey and asked to prioritize news over the large evidence/coverage stack. This is a frontend reorganization of existing capabilities. Read the [presentation contract](../../features/reading-journey.md).

## What changed

- Overview is the initial company view. Financials, News & discussion, Outlook and Compare & value follow it. Management guidance and analyst forecasts now sit in Outlook, separately from news-extracted expectations; reviewed peers sit beside valuation. No provider or model method changed.
- Recent headlines come first in News & discussion. Stories show dates, named publishers and short previews, with complete saved text available through the headline. The optional AI briefing and research context are expandable. Sentiment follows, initially filtered to news.
- Each interpreted story has an Inspect evidence dialog. The panel’s Evidence dialog separates the analysed sample, currently selected raw texts and history. Exact quotations, parent context, comparison reports, limits and original-source actions remain available. Current raw texts do not borrow old labels.
- Data & sources replaces the primary Source coverage tab and the large inline collection cards. Connection/collection details and the source library remain inspectable. Relevant unavailable, changed, stale, withheld and earlier-method states remain near the affected reading.
- My research replaces the permanent idea sidebar and holds questions, answers, saved reasoning and decisions. The workspace retains a short question summary. Question drafts survive closing/reopening and switching tabs; company changes unmount the form. The price chart starts collapsed, while the quote stays visible. Progress defaults to compact for new preferences and retains existing choices.
- Watch controls open from Watch · on/off. Reading the dialog does not write settings. Its explicit changes use the original routes. Financial highlight/position evidence opens in dialogs too. Native dialog behavior is supplemented by an explicit Tab boundary; Escape, nested source dialogs and focus return remain supported.
