# Sentiment specificity and a readable source baseline — phase 24

2 October 2026. Parent-authored review from five perspectives, not an independent consultant panel or participant study. This phase strengthens the user's prioritised news/social sentiment and alerts. It does not complete the entire product roadmap.

## Result

The earlier Amazon financing report was classified as negative without supplied evidence of harm. Prompt `thesis-source-sentiment-5` now distinguishes descriptive transactions from expressed evaluation or reported impact, while keeping explicit bullish/bearish social opinions directional and attributed. The installed model and coverage-counting policy stay unchanged. The original failure and all earlier analyses remain intact.

Users can switch to **Original sources** inside either the news or Reddit sample to read the same selected texts without AI labels or grouping. Texts judged unrelated remain visible. Publisher, date and source inspection remain available. This is a transparent reading baseline, not a new feed or additional acquisition. Complete-source access checks apply to both views.

A separate alert guard requires equal nonempty prompt, model and summary policy before describing an aggregate reversal. New analysis methods cannot themselves create a sentiment-reversal alert. Older results display a method warning; they are not automatically rerun or relabelled.

## Five-perspective review

| Perspective | Judgment | Remaining work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product | The revision addresses a concrete false adverse reading and makes the AI's interpretation directly inspectable | Validate whether the selected alerts help users revisit their own questions, rather than infer usefulness from item counts |
| UX | Separate news/social channels and a same-sample original view make scope and attribution clearer | Phone reading is lengthy; actual beginner comprehension and preference still need participants |
| Engineering | Immutable records, complete-packet withholding and method-compatible reversal detection preserve the existing contracts | No schema or new library is needed; maintain cache identity and prevent automatic paid reanalysis on upgrades |
| Research | Prewritten semantic checks and explicit contrast cases distinguish tone from predicted financial impact | Keep original failures; these reused/development cases do not establish broad accuracy, sarcasm handling or prospective alert recall |
| Business | Existing Deus/Kestrel adaptations, sources and OpenAI budget support the improvement without another key | Batch test cost is not per-customer economics. Availability of social sources, willingness to return and willingness to pay remain unresolved |

Next bounded work should test alerts across realistic sequences of unchanged, new, corrected and withdrawn public evidence against saved reasoning, retaining quiet and failed checks. Broader guidance/consensus, comments/full articles, other social platforms, financial trends and reverse valuation remain roadmap gaps. Production hosting, billing and external messages remain outside the local pitch scope.
