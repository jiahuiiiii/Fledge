The final Google revenue proposal is a retained semantic limitation. Valid IDs, exact quotes, complete grammar and human approval mechanics do not establish a correct criterion. The app must not automatically trust or activate generated checks. The current generator may still suggest an overbroad condition; the before/after review is substantive, not ceremonial.

## Corrections and preservation

- Prompt `thesis-condition-proposals-2` distinguishes prices/offers, paying customers, active paid contracts and recognized revenue. Partial completion must be explicit in the event description as well as its evidence requirement.
- Prompt `thesis-condition-proposals-3` requests concise complete criteria. Code rejects unfinished or ellipsis-bearing evidence requirements before publishing any suggestions. This is a completeness check, not a semantic-entailment guarantee.
- Earlier development bundles were rejected through append-only decisions and remain visible in history. No failed response, paid call or original saved revision was deleted or relabelled as correct.
- The original Google idea remains revision 1, draft, with its three prior private comparisons. Its two current pending suggestions are unselected and require the user to choose a report window and explicitly review/save.
- A real-company QA export retains both approved numeric suggestions, their quotations and the exact resulting revision. Read, approval, rejection and export checks made no model requests.
