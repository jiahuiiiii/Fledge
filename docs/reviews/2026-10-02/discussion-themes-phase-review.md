# Phase 33 — discussion themes

This is a parent-authored review from five professional perspectives, not five independent consultants or participant evidence.

| Perspective | Purpose and critique | Decision implemented |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product | Tone counts leave users asking what the discussion is about. More prose alone could add noise. | Optional, bounded issue themes inside the existing sentiment view, with source inspection and unknowns. |
| Investment research | Positive/negative baskets, forced balance and transferred counterparty facts can mislead. | Separate scopes; same-issue differing views only; preserve attribution, conditional plans and current versus past attitude. No investment verdict. |
| Data/ML | Valid IDs do not establish semantic support; upstream relevance selection can omit important themes. | Exact quotations and scope validation plus predeclared actual-source/authored evaluation; disclose selection dependence and avoid accuracy claims. |
| Architecture | A second autonomous process would complicate alerts and spending. | One explicit request through the existing cumulative ledger, immutable exact-analysis history and source withdrawal; no watch or classifier changes. |
| UX/commercial | A new panel could overwhelm students and does not prove willingness to pay. | Collapsed entry, two themes per source type, progressive quotation detail, mobile/history/export checks. Participant and paid-value validation remain open. |

## Initial actual-case correction

The first Microsoft response was retained and rejected before publication because the initial validator forbade all social title citations. The response used a meaningful Reddit title together with body evidence. Review also found a news summary adding details from uncited passages. Method 2 permits meaningful Reddit titles only alongside body evidence, removes generic HN titles from model passages, and directs short views supported by their specific citations. The same three source packets and twelve original criteria were frozen again before explicit calls; there was no automatic retry or source replacement. The original method, response and settled charge remain in the evidence folder.

## Semantic review after method 2

All three method-2 requests completed and passed reference validation. Broader semantic review still found a NVIDIA differing view that paired doubts about GPU economics with generic AI persistence/historical-growth optimism, dropped a “possibly” qualifier, and included a revenue assertion outside the selected quotations. The authored contrast also introduced “Board” in a heading without source support. None of these method-2 readings was promoted into product research. These are retained quality failures beyond checking the twelve selected criteria; citation validity alone was not treated as sufficient.

Method 3 requires neutral topic headings, preserves qualifiers, excludes generic AI usefulness/stock performance as rebuttal to concrete economics, avoids collective attribution implying each source said everything, and allows a fourth evidence passage where needed. The exact same source packets and original criteria were frozen before explicit tests. The 97 focused theme/sentiment/budget checks pass after the correction. This is prompt refinement on observed development failures, not an independent benchmark.
