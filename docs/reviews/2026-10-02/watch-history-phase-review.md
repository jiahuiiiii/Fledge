# Phase 21 — explain every scheduled watch check

This is a parent-authored review through five professional perspectives, not an independent consultant panel or participant research. Alerts and separate news/social sentiment remain the product priority. The gap addressed here was that the latest watch status overwrote the previous attempt, making a quiet check difficult to distinguish from lost or failed work.
