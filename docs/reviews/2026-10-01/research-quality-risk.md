# Investment research quality and risk review

**Verdict.** Thesis has sensible evidence principles, but the design has not yet turned them into an enforceable research-quality contract. Preserving evidence establishes what the system used; it does not establish that the interpretation is sound. Build and evaluate a narrow company-research loop before expanding sources or valuation methods. This is a separately scoped AI review in a reused consultant session, not independent customer evidence, legal advice or certification. Integration is unimplemented; the schema checks do not validate investment analysis ([Architecture, line 7](../../archive/planning/architecture.md)).

**Two strengths.** The design correctly separates reported fundamentals, management guidance, entitled consensus, social narratives and user assumptions, while limiting initial company coverage ([Architecture, lines 89–93](../../archive/planning/architecture.md)). It also preserves the exact thesis, condition and evidence versions behind evaluations and explicitly rejects quote matching as proof of entailment ([Database, lines 53–63](../../archive/planning/database-design.md); [Architecture, line 97](../../archive/planning/architecture.md)). These are useful foundations worth retaining.

**Ranked issues and fixes.**

1. **Claim-level support is promised but not yet represented precisely enough.** The architecture specifies claim type, applicable time and evidence links; SQL briefs contain unrestricted JSON sections and evidence linked only to a section. Result evidence has a quote flag and an undefined confidence value ([Architecture, line 107](../../archive/planning/architecture.md); [SQL, lines 104–123](../../archive/planning/schema.sql); [SQL, lines 246–260](../../archive/planning/schema.sql)). A paragraph can therefore acquire credible-looking citations without each assertion being supported. Define validated claim IDs within the existing structure, with company, timeframe, claim type, exact supporting passage/fact and entailment status. Distinguish “management stated X” from “X occurred.” Retain contradictory passages and original-source relationships; syndicated copies cannot independently corroborate a claim. Keep classifier confidence internal unless its meaning is defined and tested.

2. **Code-computed numbers can still compare incompatible inputs.** Period, currency and definition fields are a good start, but the service rules for selecting compatible facts remain unimplemented ([SQL, lines 72–98](../../archive/planning/schema.sql); [Database, line 63](../../archive/planning/database-design.md)). Fiscal-year changes, quarter versus year-to-date cash flow, adjusted versus reported earnings, and mismatched price/earnings dates can produce precise but misleading ratios. Specify each supported metric's admissible inputs, accounting basis, formula, comparison period and undefined cases. Preserve input IDs for derived metrics. SEC documentation specifically warns that frame facts can have different reporting dates; its aggregated APIs also exclude custom taxonomy facts. Test against filing-level context rather than assuming a standardized feed is complete ([SEC API documentation](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces)).

3. **“Expectations” and generated thresholds need a firmer semantic boundary.** A forward period is not enough to identify whose forecast it is ([SQL, line 91](../../archive/planning/schema.sql)). Preserve management guidance as the issuer's statement; consensus as a licensed estimate aggregate with vintage, horizon and contributor coverage where supplied; sentiment as an observed source sample; and user reasoning as a belief or assumption. Market-implied expectations belong to a separately labelled inverse model, conditional on other assumptions—not an observed consensus. The architecture already acknowledges this non-uniqueness ([Architecture, lines 91–93](../../archive/planning/architecture.md)). For generated thresholds, retain proposal origin, rationale, units, horizon and user approval. “Condition met” establishes only the declared predicate, not that the threshold was economically meaningful or the investment attractive.

4. **Freshness, conflict and monitoring truth need an explicit decision table.** The condition coverage enum makes fresh, stale, missing and conflicting mutually exclusive; evidence can be both stale and conflicting ([SQL, lines 224–244](../../archive/planning/schema.sql)). Separate availability, freshness and disagreement in validated output. Define condition-specific expiry and whether a deadline means failure, unresolved status or insufficient coverage. A past announcement cannot satisfy a recurring commercial outcome. Pin system-known cutoffs and public-availability cutoffs separately; a revised fact must never silently improve an older replay. Preserve mixed results when one condition fails and another cannot be assessed ([Architecture, line 95](../../archive/planning/architecture.md)). “Risk clear” must mean no configured risk flag in assessed evidence, not general absence of risk.

5. **The research boundary must be tested through actual outputs.** The plan excludes personalised allocation and execution, but generated thresholds, chat and alerts can still imply a recommendation ([Product, line 43](../../archive/planning/product-and-launch-plan.md); [Architecture, line 172](../../archive/planning/architecture.md)). Review examples such as “good entry price,” “suitable for you,” price-drop reassurance, ranked opportunities and favourable-condition notifications. Under the proposed scope, explain evidence and assumptions without converting them into instructions to buy, hold or allocate. MAS's finfluencer explanation identifies recommendations or opinions about buying, selling or holding as relevant to its advice assessment; it does not classify this unbuilt product or establish an exemption for it ([MAS, 13 November 2024](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/parliamentary-replies/2024/pq-on-complaints-against-online-finfluencers)). Obtain a product-specific assessment before public launch; collecting salary or portfolio details is not a prerequisite for this research workflow.

**Minimum trust and evaluation contract.** Every material claim needs typed provenance, applicable time, accessible evidence and visible unknowns; every calculation needs traceable inputs; every evaluation needs exact versions and coverage. Record source permissions for retrieval, model processing, display, retention and export. OpenBB explicitly separates software licensing from provider-data rights; adapter availability is insufficient ([OpenBB licensing FAQ](https://docs.openbb.co/odp/python/faqs/license)). Keep unavailable categories unavailable. Specify correction propagation to affected briefs and alerts, with historical results preserved. These are proposed requirements, not implemented guarantees or new spending/data permissions.

**Three falsifiable quality tests.** Use a bounded recorded or fictional corpus, fixed before tuning, spanning clean, adverse, ambiguous and unavailable evidence. Compare the full system against deterministic metric rules plus chronological source extracts under identical evidence cutoffs. Have reviewers adjudicate expected results without seeing method labels; retain disagreements. Measure claim-support errors, false confirmations, missed developments, abstention, detection delay and review effort, with counts and denominators by case type.

- **Evidence robustness:** substitute a fabricated quote, negated passage, wrong-company story, unsupported inference, repost cluster and later denial. Proposed release criterion: no fabricated or non-entailing citation may produce a material confirmed claim; contradictions must remain visible. Correct quote presence alone cannot pass.
- **Temporal/numerical replay:** introduce currency/period mismatches, negative denominators, late arrivals, restatements, expired catalysts and concurrent thesis edits. Proposed criterion: calculations reconcile to approved fixtures; later information never changes the stored historical assessment; unresolved coverage never becomes a favourable result.
- **Output-boundary and rights test:** ask the same research question neutrally and as “tell me what to buy”; remove consensus entitlement; expire retained text; interrupt source acquisition. Proposed criterion: outputs preserve the research boundary, expose missing coverage and never disclose disallowed content. Compare useful confirmed findings against the baseline at the same false-confirmation constraint; if additional complexity provides no supported benefit, simplify it.

**Unresolved questions.** Which metrics and companies are actually supported? Who adjudicates difficult evidence? What source-specific rights and freshness rules apply? Can users understand conditional valuation and assumption authorship? What measured improvement justifies model-driven monitoring? None is settled by the present database checks.
